Electrical/register checks
01 ASG: From an FPGA/server restart, load a sine configuration without pressing
   Setup first. Inspect internal out1 and physical OUT1 -> ADC at 1 MHz and
   2 MHz at a nonclipping amplitude. Verify the cycle boundary is smooth and
   the extra harmonic comb disappears. Check both ASG channels independently.
02 ASG: Off -> Trigger, trigger-source changes, frequency tuning and noise ->
   sine. Verify output enable, phase wrap, no random-phase mode on sine and
   no unexpected resets of the other channel. Noise amplitude is RMS before
   waveform clipping; use a small amplitude when measuring its distribution.
03 Filters: Request 1 MHz on a PID inputfilter; compare the returned cutoff,
   encoded filter word and measured response. Repeat an IQ HPF/LPF and the
   IIR inputfilter, using the capability constants on the loaded FPGA.
04 IIR: Restore a YAML design using zeros/poles, compare coefficients and the
   measured transfer function with original PyRPL. Verify unsupported-IIR
   bitfiles fail rather than accepting writes to a fictitious coefficient RAM.
05 Trigger/IQ: Restore named states and YAML branches. Check Trigger arms and
   IQ setup phase-aligns the IQs. Ordinary frequency edits must not phase-reset
   other IQ modules. Confirm armed status clears in both browsers on triggering.
06 Lockbox: Set a known integrator offset; Unlock/reset_offset must clear the
   actual PID accumulator. Apply additional_filter and extra_module=iir and
   verify routing/filter registers and measured loop response. Compare gains
   for equivalent V/V and mV/V configurations and physical-unit model settings.
07 Persistence: Change Lockbox threshold, calibration, output setup and a
   multi-stage sequence. Restart and reload a named state. Verify every saved
   value, and verify unrelated legacy/custom YAML keys survive an ordinary edit.
08 Logical signals/calibration: Select a lockbox.inputs.* and outputs.* signal
   in Scope/PID/IQ. Check the resolved physical routing. Check calibrated ASG/
   IQ frequencies against the configured redpitaya.frequency_correction.

Acquisition/observer checks
09 Scope: Use an external trigger that arrives several seconds late and a
   post-trigger delay much longer than the capture duration. Verify one arm
   remains pending and eventually produces a trace; Stop/Setup remains usable.
10 Scope: Average several single-shot captures; after completion open/reload an
   observer. Both browsers must show the same samples and average count. Change
   an observer's plot width/sample count and verify no extra hardware arm occurs.
11 Spectrum: Begin with Scope rolling enabled, then start a narrow-span spectrum.
   Verify it uses complete triggered captures. Average several records, close
   every browser while running, and reconnect after completion. Verify the same
   result; change only the observer's window/units/zoom and compare power and
   cross-spectrum phase against an independent reference FFT.
12 NA: Sweep low -> high frequency with auto bandwidth and a high RBW ceiling;
   bandwidth should rise at higher frequencies. Edit a continuous sweep setting
   and verify restart. Pause must mute the drive without losing the point or
   averages; Resume continues. Reconnect after a single sweep and compare all
   measured points. Confirm release of the assigned IQ after Stop/completion.
13 Ownership: With Spectrum/NA/Lockbox occupying a module, attempts to load its
   manual saved state or apply its YAML branch must fail without register writes.
   Observer plot controls and resizing remain usable, while hardware controls
   stay read-only and display the controller's current state.
14 Performance: At the intended capture interval and observer count, record ARM
   CPU/RAM, frame rate, network load and control-response time, especially with
   Spectrum trace averaging enabled. Test Save Curve during acquisition. Confirm
   acquisition and final stored/displayed averages are independent of dropped
   render frames. Server-side averages intentionally continue without viewers.
